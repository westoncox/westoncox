- **Picture-day kiosk:** an installable web app (PWA) on tablets, in English and Spanish. Families choose
  packages, add-ons, custom shirts and merchandise.
- **Payments:** card payments through Square, plus cash, check and other options. Customers get email
  confirmations.
- **Admin dashboard:** events, teams, orders and fulfillment in one place, with a portal for coaches.
- **Built with:** Next.js, React, Supabase (Postgres), Square, Resend and Vercel.
